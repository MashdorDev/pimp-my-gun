Pimp My Gun, the Flash gun builder

Pick receivers, barrels, stocks, grips, handguards, magazines, sights and scopes from a library of real-world guns (M4, M16, AK, SCAR, HK416, MP5, SVD and more), drag them together into your own custom rifle, and save it as a JPG. It runs in Ruffle, a Flash emulator, so no plugin is needed. Click the game once to unmute it.

This is not my game. Pimp My Gun was made by Doctor Noob, and every right to it belongs to its creator. It is kept here only to preserve it, so it doesn't become lost media now that Flash is gone from browsers, and it is hosted with permission.
